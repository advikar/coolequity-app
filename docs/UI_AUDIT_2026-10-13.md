# UI and feature audit · 2026-10-13

Scope: every interactive option of the map page, driven programmatically in a browser with
state checked after each action, on all five study areas; the phone layout (375 px), the
chooser, a guide and a cooling directory; a crawl of every internal link and anchor in the
built site. Build `27993d1` plus the street-room change.

## Result

**No functional defects found.** Every control did what its label says, on every city, with
no script errors. Three small issues and a short list of improvements follow.

## Issues found

1. **Console 404 on cities without a ward file.** The optional ward fetch logged a 404 in West
   Contra Costa and Pittsburg. Fixed in this change: the fetch only runs where the city declares
   a district unit.
2. **Detail header wraps to five lines on a phone.** "Rank 1 of 3,767 · need index 100 · Ward 1 ·
   Bakersfield · area #2223 · Likely rank 1–27 …" is a lot for 375 px. Not broken, but the
   ward addition lengthened it. Suggest a two-line form on narrow screens: rank and index on
   one line, ward and city on the next, the stability range as its own row.
3. **Test residue persists per browser.** Field-check notes and the shortlist live in local
   storage by design, so anything a demo operator tries stays on that machine. Fine for
   planners; for a shared demo laptop, add "Clear my notes and shortlist" under Reset.

## Improvements worth making

- **Need-versus-room scatter in the briefing.** The two numbers now exist on every area; one
  chart, top-right quadrant highlighted, is the picture a council reads fastest.
- **"Off-street" room band.** Areas with no mapped street but residents and low canopy are
  where the answer is parks, schools or private land. A fourth word would send planners there
  instead of reading "none" as "nothing to do".
- **Compare mode for wards.** The shortlist's Compare already exists; a ward-to-ward compare of
  need, residents and starred trees is the same table, two columns.
- **Print pagination.** The briefing's structure is verified; its printed page breaks with the
  By ward page are not. One print to PDF from a real browser settles it.
- **Phone detail header** as in issue 2.
- **Performance is fine** (page ready well under a second locally; the Bakersfield data file is
  4.5 MB, the largest). If a city grows past about 10 MB, serve the GeoJSON gzipped or split
  the stability fields into a second file loaded on demand.

## Not covered
