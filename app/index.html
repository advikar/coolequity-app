How to use this map

  1. Each hexagon is one area of about …. Darker means more need for shade and cooling.
  2. Click an area on the map or in the list to see its numbers, why it ranks there, and what planting trees could do. ☆ stars it onto a shortlist you can export on its own.
  3. 1 What matters most re-ranks 2 Priority areas right below it. Map display changes only what the map shows. Export & share makes a spreadsheet, a briefing PDF or a link to this view.

Save your scenario before leaving?

You changed the priorities or set planting shares. A scenario file lets you or a colleague reopen these priorities and planting shares later.

Clear the shortlist?

This removes every starred area. The areas stay in the ranking; only the shortlist and its totals are cleared.
